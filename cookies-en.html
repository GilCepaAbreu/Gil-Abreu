Cookie policy

A clear explanation of the storage technology used by this site.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

What they areWhat we useHow to controlChanges

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, can store a preference on the device itself.

2. What this site uses

The current version of this website does not use advertising, statistics, personalisation, or social-media cookies. We do not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, AdSense, remote fonts, or embedded third-party content.

To avoid showing the privacy notice on every visit, we use a local-storage entry called cc_cookie_notice_v1. It only records that you acknowledged the notice and is strictly functional. It contains no marketing identifiers and is not sent to third parties.

As there are no optional cookies, there are no advertising or measurement categories to accept. If this configuration changes, the notice and this policy will be updated before the new technology is activated.

3. How to control your preference

You can delete this site’s storage at any time in your browser settings. If you do, the notice will be shown again on a future visit. You can also review this information through the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer of the home page.

4. Changes to this policy

If we introduce optional services that use cookies or equivalent technology, we will request prior consent where the law requires it. For privacy questions, contact geral@codecraftstudio.site.

The social-media links in the footer do not load scripts from those platforms. Data is only sent to them if you choose to open a link.
