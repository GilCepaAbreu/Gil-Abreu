Privacy policy

How we process personal data from people who visit this site or contact us.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller is CodeCraft Studio, operating in Esposende, Portugal. For any question about your personal data, contact us at geral@codecraftstudio.site or +351 926 696 208.

This policy applies to codecraftstudio.site. It does not apply to third-party sites linked from this website.

2. Data we process and why

This website is designed to collect as little data as possible. It has no contact form, client area, comments, social-media pixels, personalised advertising, or analytics tools.

  • When you contact us by email or telephone: we may process your name, contact details, message content and data required to answer your request, prepare a proposal, or perform a professional relationship.
  • Strictly necessary technical data: the hosting provider may process technical access information, such as IP address, date/time and security logs, to provide and protect the service.
  • Privacy preference: we store confirmation that you have seen the cookie notice locally in your browser. See the Cookie Policy.

3. Legal basis

We process data to respond to requests and take pre-contractual steps at your request; to perform a contract where applicable; to comply with legal obligations; or on the basis of our legitimate interest in maintaining the security of the website and communications. Where processing depends on consent, it will be requested separately and may be withdrawn at any time.

4. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may use suppliers that provide hosting, email, telecommunications, technical support or accounting services, only where necessary and subject to confidentiality and security obligations. We do not load social-media content, advertising, or third-party analytics when you open this site.

If a supplier processes data outside the European Economic Area, we will implement or require appropriate safeguards under the GDPR, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

5. Retention period

We keep messages and prospective-client data for the time needed to answer the contact and follow up on the opportunity. Data related to contracted services is retained for the time required to perform the contract and for the applicable statutory periods. Technical security logs are retained only for the strictly necessary period.

6. Your rights

Under the applicable rules, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or object to processing, as well as portability of your data where applicable. You may also withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. To exercise your rights, email geral@codecraftstudio.site.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD).

7. Security and changes

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data against unauthorised access, loss, or alteration. No transmission or system is completely secure; do not send sensitive information by email unless it is necessary.

We may update this policy when data processing or the applicable rules change. The version published on this page is the version in force.

This policy reflects the current site configuration: no analytics, pixels, or advertising. Adding forms, maps, embedded videos, chat, newsletters, analytics, or advertising requires a review before publication.
